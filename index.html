Iconic flirting lines

  • Bade bade deshon mein aisi chhoti chhoti baatein hoti rehti hain, Senorita.
  • Agar woh mujhse pyaar karti hai, toh palat ke dekhegi… palat!
  • Pyaar dosti hai.
  • Socho main idea hoti aur tum technology… hamari patang kya mast udti na!

SRK-level romantic lines

  • Pyaar toh bahut log karte hain, lekin mere jaisa pyaar koi nahi kar sakta.
  • Mohabbat mein shartein nahi hoti.

Bold, cheeky & slightly cheesy

  • Tumhare gaal ke til pe dil aa gaya hai mera.
  • Jab koi pyaar mein hota hai, toh koi sahi galat nahi hota.
  • Tum samajhti kyun nahi? Agar main tumhare saath do minute aur raha, toh mujhe tumse pyaar ho jayega.

Bollywood lines you can actually text

  • Tum meri life mein aise aaye ho jaise interval ke baad movie interesting ho jaati hai.
  • Tum Simran ho ya nahi, par mera dil toh Raj banne ki acting kar raha hai.
  • Tumse baat karna meri favourite film hai. Bas problem yeh hai ki iska next episode kab aayega, pata nahi.
  • Mujhe tumhara number nahi, tumhari life mein recurring role chahiye.
  • Tumhari smile ka trailer dekh liya. Ab full movie dekhne ka mann hai.
  • Tum online aate ho toh mera dil background mein romantic music bajane lagta hai.

The over-the-top classics (pure cheese)

  • Aapke paon dekhe, bahut haseen hain. Inhe zameen par mat utariyega, maile ho jayenge.
  • Agar tum mujhe aise hi dekhte rahe, toh mujhe tumse pyaar ho jayega.

The Bollywood tashan (full attitude)

  • Tum mujhe bhool jao yeh haq main tumhe nahi doongi.
  • Babu moshai, zindagi badi honi chahiye, lambi nahi… aur aaj ki raat toh bilkul choti nahi honi chahiye.

Short, sweet & highly filmy

  • Aati kya Khandala?
  • Rahul, naam toh suna hoga?

Meta-Bollywood (breaking the 4th wall)

  • Let's skip the boring stuff and fast-forward to our choreographed dance sequence.
